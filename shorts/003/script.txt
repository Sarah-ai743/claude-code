3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  Four rooms in the house. The map shows five.
  She checks the app again. Still five.
  A clean rectangle. A doorway. Right where the hallway wall is.
  She has lived here nine years.
  She measures the hallway. There is nothing behind it.
  The map does not change. It has been there for months.
  Then she opens the travel log.
  Twenty minutes in that room. Every night. Eleven forty.
  So she sets an alarm.
  At eleven forty the robot is on its dock. Not moving.
  The log says it is cleaning.
  [BEAT] So she cuts open the wall.
  The room is there. Exactly as mapped.
  Same size. Same doorway. No door.
  And the floor is clean.
