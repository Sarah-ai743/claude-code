The 1998 tape has a flat screen in it.
Back corner of the room. Behind the cake.
Flat. Thin. Wrong by about fifteen years.
Nobody at the party is looking at it.
He digitises it and zooms in.
The screen is lit. There is an image on it.
Beige walls. An anglepoise lamp. An open laptop.
He sits back from the screen.
[BEAT] He is sitting in that room right now.
Same angle. Same lamp. Same laptop.
He turns around. Nothing behind him.
The tape is twenty-eight years old.
And one by one, everyone at the party turns.
His mother. His uncle. A child he does not know.
All of them looking at the screen.
All of them looking at him.
