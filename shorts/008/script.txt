008 - Nineteen Ninety-Eight
============================================================
Concept:       PIX-004  (B1/G5)
Target length: 47s
Voiceover:     112 words (~37s at 3.0 w/s)
Scenes:        10

ORIGINAL FICTION. Not a real event, person, company or recording.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "The 1998 tape has a flat screen in it."  (9 words)  <-- SELECTED
      Nine words. The anachronism does all the work and needs no explaining.
  [2] "There is a television in the old tape that did not exist yet."  (13 words)
      Clearer but longer, and the phrase 'did not exist yet' explains the joke.
  [3] "Something in the corner of the old tape is showing today."  (11 words)
      Atmospheric, but 'today' is vague until the reveal.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 trusts the audience. Anyone who has seen a 1998 living room knows a flat screen does not belong in one, so the viewer catches the anomaly themselves in under two seconds, which is far stronger than being told about it.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  The 1998 tape has a flat screen in it.
  Back corner of the room. Behind the cake.
  Flat. Thin. Wrong by about fifteen years.
  He digitises it and zooms in.
  The screen is on. It is showing a room.
  Beige walls. A desk. A laptop open on it.
  [BEAT] It is this room.
  It is the room he is sitting in right now.
  Same angle. Same lamp. Same laptop.
  He looks up. Nothing behind him.
  He looks back at 1998.
  And one by one, everyone at the party turns.
  His mother. His uncle. A child he does not know.
  All of them looking at the screen.
  All of them looking at him.

8/9. SOUND AND MUSIC
------------------------------------------------------------
  Music mood: Warm nostalgic tape-saturated pad for the first fifteen seconds that curdles into a single held dissonance once the zoom begins. No music at all from 0:36, so the last eleven seconds are lamp buzz and tape noise.
  Sound bed : Camcorder warble, tape dropouts, capture-deck mechanism, anglepoise buzz, and one abrupt removal of all party sound.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. FINAL TWIST
------------------------------------------------------------
  The screen in 1998 is showing the room where the tape is being watched, which means the party was looking at the viewer twenty-eight years before he sat down.
