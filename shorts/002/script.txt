------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "The room has no camera. The footage exists."  (8 words)  <-- SELECTED
      An absence and a contradiction in eight words. No setup needed.
  [2] "He is watching the monitors. Something is filming him doing it."  (11 words)
      More atmospheric, but tells the viewer the answer a beat too early.
  [3] "The archive has a camera number that does not exist."  (10 words)
      Good procedural hook, but abstract at second zero.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 pairs an absence with a contradiction in ten words and gives the viewer nothing to resolve except by staying. Hook 2 names the observer, which is the twist, and would spend the ending in the first two seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  The room has no camera. The footage exists.
  It is in the normal archive. Normal folder. Normal file name.
  Camera fourteen.
  There are eleven cameras.
  He checks the wall. No lens. No cable. No hole.
  He opens the file. It is him, from behind, watching the monitors.
  Tonight. Ten minutes ago.
  He turns around. Nothing there.
  He turns back, and the footage turns with him.
  [BEAT] Then he sees the monitors inside the footage.
  They are showing this room. From the same angle.
  So he zooms in. And in. And in.
  Every screen holds the room again, one layer deeper.
  He counts eleven layers.
  On the eleventh, the chair is empty.
  And the door is open.
