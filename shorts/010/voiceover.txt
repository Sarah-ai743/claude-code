Thirty-one people walked in. Nobody walked out.
A year of doorbell footage. She checks all of it.
Deliveries. A plumber. Her sister, twice.
She counts them twice. Thirty-one.
Every one of them walks up and goes inside.
The porch is always empty again afterwards.
No exits. Not one.
Not in a single year.
So she checks herself.
She arrives every evening at six.

The screen shows an empty porch.
Then the front door closes behind somebody.
She is standing on the path.
The door is already shut.
