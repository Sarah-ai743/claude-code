  [1] "Thirty-one people walked in. Nobody walked out."  (7 words)  <-- SELECTED

  [2] "A year of doorbell footage and not one departure."  (9 words)

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 is a complete story in eight words: a number, an action, and an impossible absence. It needs no device knowledge, no setup, and no second sentence to become unsettling.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  Thirty-one people walked in. Nobody walked out.
  A year of doorbell footage. She checks all of it.
  Deliveries. A plumber. Her sister, twice.
  She counts them twice. Thirty-one.
  Every one of them walks up and goes inside.
  The porch is always empty again afterwards.
  No exits. Not one.
  Not in a single year.
  So she checks herself.
  She arrives every evening at six.
  [BEAT] She has never been recorded leaving either.
  She steps outside with the live feed open.
  The screen shows an empty porch.
  Then the front door closes behind somebody.
  She is standing on the path.
  The door is already shut.
  And the camera says the house is occupied.
