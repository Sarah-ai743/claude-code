2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 is a complete story in eight words: a number, an action, and an impossible absence. It needs no device knowledge, no setup, and no second sentence to become unsettling.

3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  Thirty-one people walked in. Nobody walked out.
  A year of doorbell footage. She checks all of it.
  Deliveries. A plumber. Her sister, twice.
  Every one of them walks up and goes inside.
  The porch is always empty again afterwards.
  No exits. Not one. Not in a single year.
  So she slows the footage down.
  The plumber's hands are her hands.
  [BEAT] Every visitor has the same gait.
  Thirty-one arrivals. All of them her.
  Different coats. Different hours. Same shoulders.
  She checks tonight's clip.
  She arrives at six, like always.
  The front door has been locked since Tuesday.
  She has not left the house today.
  And the camera is already recording the next one.
