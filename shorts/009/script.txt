------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "The listing is a photo of her living room."  (9 words)  <-- SELECTED
      Nine words, immediately visual, and the violation is obvious without explanation.
  [2] "Someone is selling her lamp. It is still on her table."  (11 words)
      Excellent contradiction, slightly longer, and names the object before the intrusion.
  [3] "The marketplace photo was taken inside her house."  (8 words)
      Strong, but 'was taken inside' is a small extra step of reasoning.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 is the only one where the viewer's own recognition does the work. A shopping listing showing someone's private living room is understood instantly and in any market, and the intrusion lands before a single fact has been explained.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  The listing is a photo of her living room.
  Her lamp. Her table. Her window behind it.
  Posted two weeks ago. The seller has no name.
  The lamp is still on the table.
  She checks the angle. It is from the doorway.
  Nobody has been in that room but her.
  So she buys it.
  [BEAT] The parcel arrives on Thursday.
  It is her lamp. The chip on the base. The taped cable.
  She turns to the table.
  The lamp there is perfect. New. Unmarked.
  So she searches the rest of the house.
  Every object she owns is in somebody's purchase history.
  Bought years ago. Delivered years ago.
  None of the buyers are her.
