005 - The Hour Ahead
============================================================
Concept:       CAM-001  (B7/G7)
Target length: 48s
Voiceover:     117 words (~39s at 3.0 w/s)
Scenes:        10

ORIGINAL FICTION. Not a real event, person, company or recording.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "The camera has already recorded tonight."  (6 words)  <-- SELECTED
      Six words, a complete impossibility, zero setup. The strongest short hook in the batch.
  [2] "At six in the evening, the camera already has tonight's footage."  (11 words)
      Clearer, but the clause order buries the strange part until the end.
  [3] "The camera records one hour of tomorrow. Every single day."  (10 words)
      Good, but explains the rule before the viewer has felt the wrongness.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 lands the impossibility inside the first second and leaves a question the viewer cannot answer without the next line. The other two are more informative and less urgent, which is the wrong trade at second zero.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  The camera has already recorded tonight.
  Two in the morning until three. Twenty-four hours early.
  Always that hour. Always correct.
  He tests it. He moves a chair at two fifteen.
  Yesterday's file already shows him doing it.
  So he watches the whole hour, over and over.
  And then he notices the angle.
  The camera is on the ceiling, in the corner.
  The footage is from the middle of the wall.
  Eye level. Four metres from where the camera is.
  [BEAT] There is no camera there.
  They open the wall on a Tuesday.
  It is an older model. Dust covered. Still powered.
  Still recording.
  It is not pointed at the room.
  It is pointed at the new camera.

4/5/6. SCENES, CAPTIONS, VISUALS
------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE  1  [ 0.0s -  2.0s]
    CAPTION : It already recorded TONIGHT
    VISUAL  : A file list where tomorrow's date already has an entry. Cursor hovering over it.
    SFX     : sharp digital sting

  SCENE  2  [ 2.0s -  7.0s]
    CAPTION : 02:00 to 03:00. ALWAYS.
    VISUAL  : Timestamp overlay ticking through the same hour. Empty office, stripped desks.
    SFX     : IR filter click, low hum

  SCENE  3  [ 7.0s - 13.0s]
    CAPTION : So he TESTS it
    VISUAL  : He drags a chair two metres to the left, checks his watch. 02:15.
    SFX     : chair legs scraping lino

  SCENE  4  [13.0s - 19.0s]
    CAPTION : YESTERDAY'S file shows it
    VISUAL  : He scrubs yesterday's recording. The chair moves on screen exactly as he just moved it.
    SFX     : scrub whine, doubled chair scrape

  SCENE  5  [19.0s - 25.0s]
    CAPTION : Then he notices the ANGLE
    VISUAL  : Split diagram: the ceiling camera's cone versus the footage's actual viewpoint.
    SFX     : drone enters, pen scratch

  SCENE  6  [25.0s - 31.0s]
    CAPTION : EYE LEVEL. Wrong wall.
    VISUAL  : He stands where the footage's viewpoint must be, palms flat on blank magnolia plaster.
    SFX     : palms on plaster, breathing

  SCENE  7  [31.0s - 37.0s]
    CAPTION : There is NO CAMERA there
    VISUAL  : Slow pan across unbroken plaster. No fittings, no cable run, no seams.
    SFX     : silence except torch buzz

  SCENE  8  [37.0s - 42.0s]
    CAPTION : They open the WALL
    VISUAL  : A hammer breaks through. Dust. A dark cavity. Something inside reflects the torch.
    SFX     : plaster crack, dust fall, one hum starting

  SCENE  9  [42.0s - 46.0s]
    CAPTION : Dust covered. STILL POWERED.
    VISUAL  : An old camera in the cavity, thick with dust, its indicator light steady red.
    SFX     : electrical hum rising

  SCENE 10  [46.0s - 48.0s]
    CAPTION : Pointed at the NEW camera
    VISUAL  : Reverse angle: the old lens is aimed precisely at the modern camera in the ceiling corner.
    SFX     : hum cuts to total silence

------------------------------------------------------------
8/9. SOUND AND MUSIC
------------------------------------------------------------
  Music mood: Institutional quiet. A single fluorescent-pitched tone held throughout, bending very slowly downward. A second tone joins at 0:37 and the two beat against each other until the final cut.
  Sound bed : Infrared filter clicks, chair scrapes, plaster breaking, and an electrical hum that only becomes audible once the cavity is open.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. FINAL TWIST
------------------------------------------------------------
  The footage is not from the future. It is from an older camera sealed inside the wall that has been recording the new camera, and everything it sees, a day before anyone else does.
