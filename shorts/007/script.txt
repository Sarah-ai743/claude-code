007 - Significant Locations
============================================================
Concept:       PH-019  (B2/G1)
Target length: 48s
Voiceover:     113 words (~38s at 3.0 w/s)
Scenes:        10

ORIGINAL FICTION. Not a real event, person, company or recording.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "Two hundred hours at an address he's never been."  (9 words)  <-- SELECTED
      A number plus a contradiction. Nine words, instantly legible.
  [2] "His phone has a second home address."  (7 words)
      Very short and eerie, but ambiguous enough to lose some viewers.
  [3] "Every Wednesday at two in the morning his phone goes somewhere."  (11 words)
      Good rhythm, but the anomaly arrives too late in the line.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 gives the viewer a quantity to be shocked by and a contradiction to resolve, in one clause each. Hook 2 is shorter but requires the viewer to infer what a second home address means, and inference is slower than arithmetic.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  Two hundred hours at an address he's never been.
  It is in his location history. Under frequent places.
  Three hours away. Every Wednesday. Two until six.
  For two years.
  He has been asleep for every single one of them.
  So he drives out there on a Wednesday.
  It is a storage facility. Row nine. Unit forty-one.
  The rental is in his name.
  Paid monthly from an account he has never opened.
  [BEAT] His signature is on the form.
  The code on his own key ring works.
  Inside, wall to wall, floor to ceiling: phones.
  Hundreds. All his model. All charged. All unlocked.
  And every screen is showing the same thing.
  His location history.
