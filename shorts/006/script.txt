006 - Still Online
============================================================
Concept:       EXP-005  (B5/G7)
Target length: 47s
Voiceover:     114 words (~38s at 3.0 w/s)
Scenes:        10

ORIGINAL FICTION. Not a real event, person, company or recording.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "Two hundred thousand messages. Every one of them his."  (9 words)
      Good volume shock, but the impossibility is only implied.
  [2] "He boots the old computer. The conversation never stopped."  (9 words)  <-- SELECTED
      Nine words, cause and impossibility together, no technical knowledge needed.
  [3] "The machine has been in a drawer since 2001. It is still logged in."  (14 words)
      Strong, but too long to land inside the first two seconds.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 2 puts an ordinary action and an impossible result in the same breath, and the word 'stopped' implies a duration the viewer immediately wants measured. Hook 3 says the same thing with five more words, which is five words too many at second zero.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  He boots the old computer. The conversation never stopped.
  Twenty-five years in a drawer. The chatroom is still open.
  Two hundred thousand lines.
  And his name is on most of them.
  He scrolls. It is his rhythm. His spelling mistakes.
  But it is not his life.
  A line from 1999 about a job he took last year.
  A line from 2003 about his daughter. She is six.
  [BEAT] The words he uses only started existing recently.
  He types hello.
  The room answers before he presses enter.
  It has been holding the conversation the whole time.
  Everything he has said online since is the echo.
  He is not catching up.
  He is three seconds behind.

    CAPTION : Everything since is the ECHO
    VISUAL  : Modern phone screen beside the CRT, showing the same words a beat later.
    SFX     : doubled notification, phase offset

  SCENE 10  [45.0s - 47.0s]
    CAPTION : Three seconds BEHIND
    VISUAL  : The CRT cursor blinks. The phone's cursor blinks three seconds later. Black.
    SFX     : two clicks out of sync, cut to silence

------------------------------------------------------------
8/9. SOUND AND MUSIC
------------------------------------------------------------
  Music mood: A single warm analogue drone that sounds like hardware rather than music, sitting just under the CRT hum. It drops out entirely at 0:31 and does not return, so the last sixteen seconds run on room tone and cursor clicks.
  Sound bed : CRT degauss, fan hum, mechanical keyboard, and a deliberate three-second delay applied to every modern sound in the final shots.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. FINAL TWIST
------------------------------------------------------------
  The chatroom is not an old conversation still running. It is the live one, and everything the man has said online for twenty-five years has been a delayed copy of it.
