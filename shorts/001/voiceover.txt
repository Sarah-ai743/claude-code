The video gets one frame longer every play.
Same file. Same size. Same checksum.
One more frame.
He plays it eleven times. Eleven new frames.
They are all at the end. And they are not noise.
It is the room where the video was filmed.
Empty. Still. The light through the window keeps changing.
So he plays it again. And again. Four hundred times.
The room stays empty for ten thousand frames.
[BEAT] Then the door opens.
Someone sits down. Faces the lens. Waits.
The room only moves when somebody is watching.
He stops playing it.
So now it is your turn.
There is only one way to find out what he does next.
