001 - One Frame Longer
============================================================
Concept:       PIX-002  (B5/G6)
Target length: 48s
Voiceover:     114 words (~38s at 3.0 w/s)
Scenes:        10

ORIGINAL FICTION. Not a real event, person, company or recording.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALTERNATIVE OPENING HOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------
  [1] "The video gets one frame longer every play."  (8 words)  <-- SELECTED
      Eight words. States the anomaly and implicates the viewer in it.
  [2] "He has played the file eleven times. It is eleven frames longer."  (12 words)
      Stronger specificity, but spends a second on the man before the strangeness.
  [3] "The file never changes size. It keeps getting longer."  (9 words)
      Nice contradiction, but needs a beat of technical understanding to land.

2. HOOK SELECTION
  Hook 1 is the only one that is fully legible before the viewer has decided to scroll. It contains the anomaly noun, a complete impossibility, and a second-person address that makes the viewer complicit in the mechanism that drives the story.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOICEOVER
------------------------------------------------------------
  The video gets one frame longer every play.
  Same file. Same size. Same checksum.
  One more frame.
  He plays it eleven times. Eleven new frames.
  They are all at the end. And they are not noise.
  It is the room where the video was filmed.
  Empty. Still. The light through the window keeps changing.
  So he plays it again. And again. Four hundred times.
  The room stays empty for ten thousand frames.
  [BEAT] Then the door opens.
  Someone sits down. Faces the lens. Waits.
  The room only moves when somebody is watching.
  He stops playing it.
  So now it is your turn.
  There is only one way to find out what he does next.

  SCENE  1  [ 0.0s -  2.0s]
    CAPTION : One frame LONGER

    SFX     : tape hiss, distant room tone

  SCENE  5  [17.0s - 23.0s]
    CAPTION : EMPTY. Still. Waiting.
    VISUAL  : Time-lapse of the window light shifting across the empty floor while the frame counter climbs.
    SFX     : rising low drone under a ticking frame counter

  SCENE  6  [23.0s - 28.0s]

    VISUAL  : His face in monitor light, unblinking, as reflected frame counts race in his glasses.
    SFX     : click loop compressed into a stutter

  SCENE  7  [28.0s - 33.0s]

    VISUAL  : Frame counter racing past ten thousand over the unchanging empty room.
    SFX     : counter whirr, everything else muted

  SCENE  8  [33.0s - 39.0s]
    CAPTION : Then the DOOR opens
    VISUAL  : In the footage, the far door swings inward. Half a second of total silence before it moves.
    SFX     : total silence, then a single wooden creak

  SCENE  9  [39.0s - 44.0s]
    CAPTION : It moves when you WATCH
    VISUAL  : A figure sits in a chair in the centre of the room and turns to face the lens. Held still.
    SFX     : sub-bass swell, room tone drops out

  SCENE 10  [44.0s - 48.0s]
    CAPTION : YOUR turn.
    VISUAL  : Cut to black except the video player, paused, with the replay button pulsing. Duration ticks up once more.
    SFX     : one dry click, then silence
